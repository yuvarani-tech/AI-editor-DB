About VoiceSuite

VoiceSuite is a modern, AI-powered text editor designed to bridge the gap between spoken word and written text.

Seamless Transcription

Utilizing the Web Speech API, VoiceSuite accurately transcribes your spoken words in real-time, allowing for a distraction-free writing experience.

Persistent Cloud Storage

Backed by a robust PHP/MySQL infrastructure, your notes and personalized settings are securely saved and accessible across your devices.

Dynamic Glassmorphism UI

Enjoy a premium, highly responsive user interface with customizable themes, smooth animations, and a distraction-free environment.
